SecretHub public trust data and packaging notices
================================================

The Mozilla-derived public TLS root bundle is supplied by the locked CAStore
dependency, in castore/priv/cacerts.pem. Its original PEM provenance header is
retained without modification. SecretHub reports the SHA-256 of those actual
packaged bytes; the source-conversion hash in the PEM header is separate.

Mozilla/NSS source data
----------------------
The bundle's recorded source is Mozilla/NSS certdata.txt, converted with
mk-ca-bundle.pl. The Mozilla source carries this notice:

This Source Code Form is subject to the terms of the Mozilla Public
License, v. 2.0. If a copy of the MPL was not distributed with this
file, You can obtain one at http://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/.

Source: https://github.com/nss-dev/nss/blob/master/lib/ckfw/builtins/certdata.txt
Conversion provenance: https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html

Certificates remain attributable to their issuing organizations. This bundle
does not reproduce all Firefox trust policy and does not authorize SecretHub
Agent, application or management client identities.

Let's Encrypt staging issuance roots
------------------------------------
The separately packaged staging roots are used only to validate staging-issued
chains. Their official source URLs, retrieval URLs and exact PEM/DER hashes are
recorded in letsencrypt-staging-manifest.json beside this notice. They are not
added to the default public TLS bundle or internal identity trust.
